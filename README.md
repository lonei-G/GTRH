GTRH is an unofficial add-on for GTNH that brings back the "heroes" lost to older versions.

> [!NOTE]
> This is an unofficial mod. Please avoid discussing this mod in official GTNH forums.

## Features

- **Disassembler** — twelve single-block machines that take items apart into their components.
- **Processing Array** — runs up to 64 single-block GregTech machines placed in its controller in bulk.
- **Processing Center (Nine in One)** — a masterpiece inherited from GT++, the highest mountain of parallelism.
- **Unbreakable Lightning Rods** — lightning rods are no longer destroyed when struck by lightning.
- **Infinite ME Output Hatches** — ME output buses and hatches have unlimited cache capacity by default.
- **Indium Comb Processing** — adds Large Chemical Reactor recipes for indium combs.
- **QFT Waterline** — the QFT waterline recipes.
